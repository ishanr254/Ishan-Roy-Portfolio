### The Competition

The [AIAA Design Build Fly](https://www.aiaa.org/dbf) competition challenges collegiate teams to design, build, and fly a radio-controlled aircraft in a series of missions and following a series of design constraints released each fall. The competition is scored based on both **flight mission performance** and a **technical report**, providing students with the challenge of not only creating a high-performance aircraft but also substantiating it in a 60-page technical report.
